# Self-Care Products

## Environmental Impact

Self-care and personal care products — skincare, haircare, grooming, and hygiene items — generate environmental impact across two primary dimensions: formulation and packaging. At the category level, single-use plastic packaging is a dominant concern, with the global personal care sector producing billions of plastic units annually, many of which are too small or contaminated to be practically recycled.
