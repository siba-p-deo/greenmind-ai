# Watches & Wearables

## Environmental Impact

This category spans two distinct segments with very different environmental profiles. Traditional mechanical and quartz watches have minimal ongoing environmental impact and can last decades. Smartwatches and fitness trackers share the consumer electronics manufacturing footprint — lithium batteries, circuit boards, and sensors — combined with short effective lifespans driven by software support cycles and battery degradation. At the category level, the growth of the smartwatch and fitness tracker segment has introduced a high-turnover electronics product into a category previously associated with long-term ownership.

## Typical Lifespan

A quality mechanical watch can function for generations with periodic servicing. Quartz watches typically last ten to twenty years. Smartwatches and fitness trackers have effective lifespans of three to five years, constrained by battery degradation and software support timelines.

## Repairability

Traditional watches have a well-established servicing and repair ecosystem. Battery replacement in quartz watches is low-cost and widely available. Smartwatches and fitness bands are generally difficult to repair — screens and batteries are often bonded, and manufacturer repair support is limited.

## Common Purchase Triggers

Fitness tracking feature launches, notification integration with smartphones, aesthetic novelty, and gifting occasions drive purchase in this category. Social comparison and health monitoring interest are common motivators.

## Sustainable Alternatives

Choosing a quality traditional watch over a smartwatch is the highest-impact alternative in this category. For fitness tracking, assessing whether existing smartphone sensors meet needs before purchasing dedicated hardware is a useful first step.

## End-of-Life Considerations

Smartwatches require e-waste processing. Traditional watches can be serviced, resold, or inherited. Battery disposal should follow local regulations.

## GreenMind Guidance

This information is category-level. A mechanical or quartz watch bought once and maintained will have a fraction of the lifetime environmental impact of cycling through multiple smartwatch generations.
