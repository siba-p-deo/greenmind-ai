# Home Appliances

## Environmental Impact

Home appliances — refrigerators, washing machines, dishwashers, ovens, and smaller kitchen devices — are unusual in the consumer goods landscape because their operational environmental impact over a lifetime of use can exceed their manufacturing footprint. Energy consumption during use is the dominant impact driver for large appliances. At the category level, refrigeration and laundry account for significant shares of residential electricity consumption. Refrigerants in older cooling appliances include compounds with high global warming potential. Small appliances — toasters, kettles, blenders — have lower operational impact but contribute substantially to e-waste due to high discard rates and low repairability.

## Typical Lifespan

Large appliances typically carry expected lifespans of ten to fifteen years or more. Anecdotal and industry evidence suggests actual replacement cycles are shortening. Small kitchen appliances have typical lifespans of three to eight years, with failure often driven by single components.

## Repairability

Large appliances are generally repairable — parts are manufactured and stocked for major categories, and a professional repair sector exists. The right-to-repair movement has focused significantly on this category. Small appliances are less consistently repairable, with sealed housings and unavailable spare parts common.

## Common Purchase Triggers

Appliance failure, energy efficiency upgrades, kitchen renovation, and new household formation drive the majority of large appliance purchases.

## Sustainable Alternatives

Repairing rather than replacing at first failure, choosing appliances with documented spare part availability, and selecting higher energy efficiency ratings to reduce operational impact are the most impactful approaches.

## End-of-Life Considerations

Large appliances contain refrigerants, metals, and electronic components requiring specialist collection. Many retailers and local authorities provide take-back services. Refrigerant handling must be performed by certified technicians.

## GreenMind Guidance

This information is category-level. For large appliances, a single repair that extends lifespan by three to five years typically avoids the embedded carbon of manufacturing a replacement unit.
