# Self-Care Products

## Environmental Impact

Self-care and personal care products — skincare, haircare, grooming, and hygiene items — generate environmental impact across two primary dimensions: formulation and packaging. At the category level, single-use plastic packaging is a dominant concern, with the global personal care sector producing billions of plastic units annually, many of which are too small or contaminated to be practically recycled. Certain formulation ingredients — microbeads, some UV filters, and preservatives — have documented aquatic toxicity. Fragrance ingredients are largely unregulated for environmental disclosure. Production of natural botanical ingredients can involve significant land use.

## Typical Lifespan

Individual product lifespan varies widely. Many products have functional shelf lives of twelve to thirty-six months. Overconsumption — using more product per application than necessary — is common and shortens effective lifespan of purchased items.

## Repairability

Not applicable to this category. The equivalent concept is concentration and refill: concentrated formats and refillable packaging reduce per-use waste and packaging impact.

## Common Purchase Triggers

Marketing claims around skin improvement, social media influence, gifting, routine building, and new product launches drive high purchase frequency in this category. Anxiety around appearance is a documented emotional trigger.

## Sustainable Alternatives

Refillable packaging systems, concentrated or solid formats (bars replacing liquid bottles), multi-use products, and purchasing only what is genuinely consumed before expiry reduce impact meaningfully.

## End-of-Life Considerations

Empty plastic packaging recyclability depends on local infrastructure and container material. Pump dispensers and mixed-material packaging are often non-recyclable. Some retailers offer take-back programmes.

## GreenMind Guidance

This information is category-level. Finishing existing products before purchasing new ones, and choosing refillable or solid formats, are the most practical sustainability actions in this category.
