# Shoes

## Environmental Impact

Footwear production is resource-intensive at a category level. Leather production carries a significant land use and methane footprint associated with livestock farming, as well as chemical-heavy tanning processes. Synthetic alternatives such as PU and PVC reduce animal inputs but introduce fossil-fuel-derived materials and chemical processing concerns. Most footwear combines multiple materials — rubber, foam, textile, adhesives — which complicates end-of-life processing. Manufacturing is predominantly concentrated in regions with limited environmental regulation enforcement, amplifying production impacts.

## Typical Lifespan

Quality footwear is capable of lasting five to ten years with appropriate care and resoling. Fast fashion footwear and trend-driven designs are often constructed for shorter use cycles of one to two years. Athletic footwear degrades in performance terms before structural failure.

## Repairability

Footwear repair — resoling, heel replacement, stitching — is a well-established trade with accessible services in most urban areas. Shoes with cemented rather than stitched construction are harder to resole. Higher-quality construction generally indicates better repairability.

## Common Purchase Triggers

Trend cycles, perceived wear, athletic performance marketing, social occasions, and fast fashion pricing are common purchase drivers at this category level.

## Sustainable Alternatives

Resoling existing quality footwear, purchasing secondhand, choosing stitched-construction styles from durable categories, and selecting natural materials from traceable sources are the most sustainable approaches.

## End-of-Life Considerations

Most footwear is not recyclable through standard channels due to mixed material construction. Specialist footwear take-back and material recovery programmes exist but remain limited in scale.

## GreenMind Guidance

This information is category-level. Resoling an existing quality pair of shoes is almost always more sustainable and cost-effective than purchasing a new pair.
