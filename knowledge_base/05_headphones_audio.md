# Headphones & Audio Devices

## Environmental Impact

At a category level, headphones and personal audio devices carry a manufacturing footprint tied to circuit board production, lithium battery inclusion, and plastic and metal housing fabrication. Wireless and true wireless earbuds present particular environmental concerns: their small form factor places lithium cells inside enclosures that are extremely difficult to open, making battery replacement practically inaccessible. As battery capacity degrades — typically within two to four years — the entire device is frequently discarded. The global volume of small personal audio devices contributes meaningfully to the e-waste stream.

## Typical Lifespan

Wired headphones without active electronics can last a decade or more with basic cable care. Wireless headphones typically have a functional lifespan of three to five years before battery degradation meaningfully impacts usability. True wireless earbuds generally have the shortest effective lifespan in this category.

## Repairability

Over-ear wired headphones represent the most repairable form factor — ear pads, cables, and headbands are commonly replaceable. Wireless devices vary; some models offer user-replaceable batteries, most do not. True wireless earbuds are largely non-repairable.

## Common Purchase Triggers

Battery decline in existing devices, audio quality comparison, new wireless standards, aesthetic preferences, and loss of small earbuds are the primary purchase drivers.

## Sustainable Alternatives

Choosing wired over wireless, selecting models with documented user-replaceable batteries, purchasing refurbished, and replacing ear pads rather than entire devices extend useful life significantly.

## End-of-Life Considerations

Audio devices contain lithium batteries and electronic components requiring e-waste processing. Batteries embedded in earbuds present particular recycling challenges.

## GreenMind Guidance

This information is category-level. Wired headphones represent the most sustainable form factor in this category by a meaningful margin.
