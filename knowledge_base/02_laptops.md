# Laptops

## Environmental Impact

Laptops share the high manufacturing footprint common to consumer electronics. Component production — particularly processors, memory, and display panels — is energy and resource intensive. At the category level, manufacturing typically accounts for the dominant share of a laptop's lifetime carbon emissions. Thin and light designs increasingly use non-standard components and sealed enclosures that reduce repairability, indirectly increasing environmental impact by shortening effective lifespan.

## Typical Lifespan

Laptops are capable of operating for six to eight years or more, though the average replacement cycle is closer to four to five years. Operating system support timelines and performance degradation relative to software demands are common drivers of early replacement.

## Repairability

Repairability varies widely across this category. Older or business-oriented models tend to offer accessible RAM, storage, and battery replacement. Ultrabook and consumer-oriented designs often use soldered components and proprietary fasteners, limiting repair options. Independent repair is viable for many models with publicly available service documentation.

## Common Purchase Triggers

Performance requirements for work or study, hardware failure, weight and portability preferences, and operating system end-of-life are the most common purchase drivers in this category.

## Sustainable Alternatives

Refurbished business-grade laptops offer strong build quality, documented repairability, and significantly lower cost than new consumer equivalents. RAM and SSD upgrades can extend an existing device's useful life considerably.

## End-of-Life Considerations

Laptops contain lithium batteries, circuit boards with heavy metals, and display panels requiring specialist handling. Certified e-waste facilities can responsibly process these materials.

## GreenMind Guidance

This information is category-level and not specific to any model. Upgrading storage or memory in an existing device is often the most sustainable and cost-effective path before considering a new purchase.
